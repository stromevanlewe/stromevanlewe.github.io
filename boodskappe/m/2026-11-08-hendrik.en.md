# As You Follow Him

*Hendrik van der Merwe · 8 November 2026*

In Esther's day the young women were prepared for a full year before they could appear before the king. Six months with oil of myrrh, six months with spices and perfumes. Only then did the officials take her to the king.

Our King does not work that way.

He does not wait until you are ready. He does not send officials to vet you. He simply says: follow Me. And He cleanses you **as you follow Him.**

Isn't that wonderful? That we can have a King who cleanses me while I walk behind Him, and not only after I have sorted myself out.

And I want to make these two points as we go through the Word: never and nowhere will I be in a position where Jesus pushes me aside if I want to love Him.

## Why do we do these things?

Let me ask you something. The things that happen in church — why do we preach? Why do we sing? Why do we come together as a community? What is the purpose of it?

If I knew nothing this morning, what is the reason we do these things?

To make His Name great. Unity. All special things. We can say many things.

But I want to ask you: **don't we all want to be like Jesus?**

That is really what we as a church should want to be. All of us want to be a better example in this world. But what it actually comes down to when you are in church is one thing: *I want to be like Jesus.*

When I played under-13 rugby there was a rugby magazine with a centre spread — one of those big ones you pull out of the book. Then I would stick those men up on my wall. And then I would walk into that room and say to myself: *I am like you.*

Didn't you do things like that? Everyone had their own way.

We *want* to be like someone, because God made us that way. You have a desire to know someone, a desire to live, a desire for companionship.

We know that out of ourselves we are not perfect. But if we pursue purity, we can get there by becoming like Jesus. How do we get there?

## Awe

I arrived at a thought: the word *awe*.

Awe is a deep, complex emotion. It holds respect together with astonishment, and also a kind of fear and reverence and humility.

When you are in awe, it stops your train of thought for a moment. You are shocked. You are astonished. You did not think this thing could happen to you.

And I want to connect that to Jesus.

> *"Confused and disturbed, Mary wondered what the angel's greeting could mean."*
> Luke 1:29

Here we see a young woman who did not expect this at all.

Who of you has ever met an angel? Me neither. I know they are there, but I think I would be startled.

Mary is an example to us of awe at God's work — of how she becomes part of a plan she did not think up herself. And in a sense we can say: through that experience the Lord emptied her of herself and filled her with *His* plans.

That is the most important thing we have to understand.

Our humanity — our view of life, what people have done to us, what is inside us — can be very good for us. But it can also be our enemy. It puts fears in me.

Because look at how the enemy works. He uses circumstances to hold us, to give us fear of things that very often are not even reality. He wants to get us to that point where we flinch and simply react to every negative thing that happens.

**But Jesus has the power.** If we really look at *Him*, He empties us of what is bad and fills us with what is new and good. He has the power to still my thoughts about what people did to me in my past.

And why does that matter?

Because how can I experience the new in life if I have not yet been set free from the old?

## What no eye has seen

> *"This is what the Scripture means when it says: What no eye has seen, what no ear has heard, what has entered no one's heart, this God has prepared for those who love Him."*
> 1 Corinthians 2:9

What is "no eye"? It is the human eye.

And notice where the verse lands: *for those who love Him.*

How can you love someone you do not know? How can you love someone you do not even know about?

There is something God has prepared for us. But it is not necessarily going to happen the way *I* want it to work out.

This is a wonderful road, because the Lord is not only going to free you from the things that hurt you in the past. **He is going to free you from yourself.** From your own thinking.

> *"The natural person cannot take hold of these truths that come from the Spirit of God. It sounds like nonsense to them. They cannot understand it, because these things must be understood from a spiritual perspective."*
> 1 Corinthians 2:14

To take hold of something means to make it part of you.

So what I want to experience of God — if I experience it *without* Jesus, or wealth without Jesus, or friends without Jesus, then it is really a temporary thing.

But if I experience Jesus, and I have friends, then I have a future. Then I have a perspective on how to build my life. Then fears are removed — because that is how the enemy holds me.

If I want to move, if I want to be free, the enemy has plenty of ammunition for as long as I have forgiven no one.

I know this: when the Lord sets you free, *then* you want to forgive. You forgive people you thought you could never forgive. Why? Because you have experienced who Jesus is.

Jesus gave you a piece of Himself. That is what He did on the cross. At the table He says: *take and eat, this is My body.*

So if we want to be like Jesus and follow Him, what we are really saying is: Lord, I want to be like You. I want to learn how to live. I want to take Your words. I want to grow.

The natural person cannot grasp it — *it's a foreign concept.* But the wonderful thing about Jesus is this: if you decide to follow Him, then **He** makes it a revelation in your life. He will be the life for you. He will be the strength. He will show the way. He will set you free.

Here we see that we as people cannot control or even follow God's life, God's kingdom, God's church with our own way of doing things.

It would not have worked anyway. How many times have we tried?

Before I began to understand what it means to follow, I gave my heart to the Lord many times. Even at school. Then the naughty mates would come and say: let's go and do this. And then I would want to say: guys, I gave my heart to the Lord on Sunday evening. And before I know it, I am doing those things anyway.

Because do you know what my idea of Jesus was? **He expects me to be good, and then I am acceptable to Him.**

While that was not the case at all.

If someone had only told me earlier: follow Him and learn who He is. The more you think about His love, the more your thoughts and your feelings and your will change — **as you follow Him.**

And the Word says it so beautifully: He will open a new and living way for you. A living way. Not a dead one. Not those old roads that hold you back and pull you back.

## Two things: believe and follow

So if we know the reason we are sitting here is to become like Jesus, what is a practical answer?

Two things: **believe** and **follow.**

Sounds simple, doesn't it? It is always these small things — like love — that sound so simple but whose effect is so large.

## One. Believe

To believe is not an opinion or a summary or an act of will about my experience of God. It is first of all an **emptying** and a **filling** with who He is.

I want to stay with *emptying* for a moment.

You are very thirsty. Have you ever been so thirsty that your tongue sticks to the roof of your mouth? If I gave you the choice between brown muddy water and a clean glass — you can see the condensation on the side, nice and cold — what are you going to drink?

The glass. Of course.

So we must remember how the Lord sees us. He does not see mud. He does not see hurt. He does not see pain.

Do you know what He shows you? **Follow Me. I am going to let you drink from this glass.**

Make an exchange. Believe Me. Trust Me to go with you all the way down to the foundation — to where the enemy began to make things wrong. We cannot go there on our own.

### Look into my eyes

My father was not a doctor, but he did those things.

I once stepped on an enormous thorn. I will never forget it. I sat flat on the ground like this, and he was lying there at my feet. And all I could see was that needle.

But do you know what he said to me?

**"Look into my eyes and be still."**

And I thought to myself: *me*, be still? This thing is going to hurt.

He pulled that thorn out.

And I can tell you — today, listen carefully, I am not talking about then, I am talking about *today* — today I cannot remember how much it hurt. But I can remember my father saying: don't look at the pain, look into my eyes. I am going to pull this thing out.

I see it as if it is happening right here.

We are limited in our thinking. We see problems and solutions in one small cramped answer: if I just get a bit more money, I will get out of my tight spot.

Do you know what Jesus sees? **Trust Me completely.**

The answer is not to have more money. The answer is: focus on Jesus, and learn in love to follow Him. He will sit with you and work this thing out alongside you.

Because what does Paul say? *For me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.* He came to know Jesus, and Jesus led him right through that valley of the shadow of death and said to him: look into My eyes, because I am here.

That is simply Jesus.

### Faith like a mustard seed

So I have to be emptied. And when those fears start gathering around me, I need to know that Jesus can replace them with faith.

And when I do that, I am like that little boy with the five loaves and two fish. If I put it in *His* hands, He lifts it to the Father and says: Lord, use this little bit for a testimony.

Because what does Jesus say? If you have faith like a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain: go and throw yourself into the sea.

And what happens when it falls into the sea? It disappears.

He says: I will remove your sins as far as the east is from the west. Who does that? Not people. **God.** And He will not remember it again.

> *"What is faith, then? It is the firm assurance that the things we look forward to will indeed happen. It is the proof that God will make real the things we cannot yet see."*
> Hebrews 11:1

Let me take one example. At one stage I could swear. I could write you poems out of my swear words. And it hurt **me** deeply. *I* want to be full of the Lord, and before I know it these sharp words come out.

And do you know what? The Lord taught me to love *Him*. Not to get a hiding every time I want to swear. *Love Jesus.*

He is not merciless while you change. He knows how to walk a road with you so that you can come to the place where you too can be like Jesus.

### Seek first the kingdom

> *"But seek first the kingdom of God and His righteousness, and all these things will be added to you."*
> Matthew 6:33

He is not going to use rose petals, the way that king did. He is going to use *His* blood. He is going to use *His* love. And as we walk faithfully, we will see the change that God brings about.

But what is our part in it? It is so beautiful: *seek first the kingdom.* Be filled with *Him* first. And then: *and His righteousness* — *His* way of doing things.

When the Lord called me, I thought: well, I have to learn these verses, and they must roll off my heart like that, and I cannot remember the things.

But what it really comes down to is this: when you experience Jesus' life and love, and you look into the Word, you say: **hey, that is what I am experiencing!**

The life is already in you because you follow Jesus, and then your thoughts are taught: oh, so *that* is what is happening.

And what does He say? *I will write My laws on your heart.* I will make you remember: that day the Lord did this. And then I share it with people — because that day I saw how great Jesus is.

And then it becomes a testimony. Then it becomes alive.

### The first pancake

Sometimes, because of the joy the Lord puts in me, I cannot keep my mouth shut. And I get myself into plenty of trouble over it.

*But it's a part of Jesus. And I will continue to do that.*

Because do you know what? Jesus looks at me and says: hey, that fellow is a bit odd. He is like the first pancake — a bit iffy.

*But he loves Me.*

## The church is not your judge

Jesus has great grace with us. For every moment of your sorrow He can give enough love that you want to hear *more* of Him, that you want to behold Him.

But deep down in that we sometimes make a choice — or the enemy lies to us.

*Don't be part of the church. These people sit and judge you. Your toes aren't straight enough. Look at those people, they knew your past.*

He will use even that.

But what do people in the church actually want to see? *They want to see the freedom of you following Jesus.*

Because when you become free with Jesus, you become beautiful to look at. The Lord will restore you, and that is what we are part of. We are part of a church that may be fallible at times, *but we have one mission: to become like Jesus.*

## Two. Follow

We do not believe out of ourselves. Jesus brings us to Himself. He reminds us of Himself.

And then comes the second thing: follow.

To follow is to be filled with the assurance of who Jesus is.

You will get momentum when you feel: well, this thing actually *works.* And then you get joy.

Your whole being — what you *are*, what you are *becoming*, what you *will be* — is filled with Jesus. You are going to be a different person when He empties you of yourself and fills you with Him.

> *"And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony, and they did not love their lives so much as to be unwilling to die for Him."*
> Revelation 12:11

There are two elements here.

You have a past from before you knew Jesus, and Jesus has to free you from it. But to be free, you have to be freed from the one who holds you in lies and in circumstances. That is the blood of the Lamb.

And then: the word of our testimony. He washes us, He teaches us, He leads us. He uses the church to make it clear, so that we can experience what a testimony is.

As we behold Jesus, a unique testimony comes to each one — to each one who follows Him and no longer gives that old life the right to stop him from seeing Jesus.

In essence He signed a contract to free us from all those powers. But now the Lord puts a heart in me that decides for itself where I will go, and what I will lay down.

And that is hard, friends. Sometimes it is hard to lay my dream down before the Lord, to say: Lord, *for that treasure of Jesus I have to give this away.*

And sometimes the Lord will not tell you "I am going to give you this if you do that."

I sometimes say to my children: if you do this, then I will give you that. But that is to inspire them, to make them believe they *can* do it. I am not manipulating them into doing something their heart does not want to do.

That is the difference.

**Jesus has better for us.** But we have to come to a point where we love Jesus more than we love ourselves. I have to come to a point where I believe: *yes, He makes me a child of God.* He makes me part of a royal priesthood, of a people who belong to Him, of a body, of a church.

## The road to Emmaus

I want to close with the men on the road to Emmaus.

Jesus died and He rose. But many people did not believe it — and that is interesting, because He *had* told His disciples that He would rise. He said it at the table. And yet, when it happened, nobody had understood His heart.

They scattered. That is exactly what was prophesied: the shepherd will be struck, and the sheep will scatter.

So when Jesus rose, He appeared physically to people, so that they would become the church and believe that He is alive. But they had to see it with their eyes.

And what happened with Thomas? He said: unless I can put my finger into His side, I will not believe. And while he is saying it, Jesus is standing next to him and says: Thomas, it is better to believe than to see.

Why?

Because **faith opens the door for God, but sight only opens the door for the flesh.**

And then there are these two men walking. They knew Jesus to some degree, and they are talking to each other on the way from Jerusalem to Emmaus: well, we don't understand these things. Jesus was supposed to redeem us. And now He is simply gone.

And Jesus appears to them and walks with them and talks with them.

When they reach the house they say: come in and eat with us. And while He is eating with them, He opens their eyes so that they can see it is *Him.*

And do you know what they say to each other once He is gone?

*The whole way He walked with us, we could not see Him. And were our hearts not burning?*

The power of the Holy Spirit lets us see Jesus in circumstances where the world cannot see Him. Where people cannot believe — there the Holy Spirit has the power to set your heart burning.

## Daddy, help me

The women in my life are the most beautiful on earth to me. Do you know why? God gave them to me to love. Not to compete with them. To find a new way every day and to look deeper into their hearts.

We struggled with the computer this morning, and I stood there and I was scratchy. And I said to my wife: don't stress like that, let's just take photos of it. You be calm.

I was irritated. But I did not try to think for her or do it for her. I simply said: be calm. It is what it is. *Let's go.*

And do you know what, sometimes that is all it takes. Sometimes that is all the Lord says to you: **follow Me.**

He says it to Peter too. Peter asks: but what about this man? And He says: follow Me. Because to everyone who follows Him, He has given the right to become children of God.

Then someone else, through your love for Jesus, can come closer to Him. Because that is what He wants. He wants disciples, people who know what it is to walk this road.

And now, before I close.

Every father wants to hear his child call out to him. It is the most beautiful thing to him.

From the time they were small I had my children ask. One of my daughters learned to say it nicely: "Daddy, may I ask..." And I say: yes, if she comes and asks.

And one day she asked: but why?

And I said: **because I want to hear her speak.**

That is all Jesus wants.

He can hear my prayer when I say: Lord, please stir someone else's heart so that he can follow You. But for *that* person to start moving, He wants to hear: *Daddy, help me with this. Teach me.*

All the Lord is saying is: take one step.

Think of those films about the men stranded on an island. There is no food. And then you see a plane go past. What are you going to do? You are going to make fire. You are going to do everything you can to be seen.

And what does the Word say?

> *"The word is near you, in your mouth and in your heart."*
> Romans 10:8

He is near.

**But you have to call Him.**

## Prayer

Lord, thank You so much for Your love and Your grace. Thank You that we do not have to meet human standards to get Your attention.

Your Holy Spirit speaks very clearly to us. We just do not always understand *how* You speak.

Change my life as You see fit. Let the people who look at me see Jesus. Make that my desire.

Make us Your followers. Make us so that we hold nothing back from Your loving hands. Because we know this: when You change us, You set us free — free from what we *thought* it means to follow Jesus, free from all these preconceived ideas of who You are.

You want us to seek You. Seek first the kingdom.

I pray that we will come free, and that You will become involved in our lives.

In Jesus' Name, amen.

>> He does not wait until you are clean to call you. He makes you clean as you follow Him.
