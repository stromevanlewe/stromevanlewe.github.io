# Hunger for Righteousness

*Hendrik van der Merwe · 4 October 2026*

I want to talk this morning about being built into the wall.

There is a wall here in the church, and it goes back a while with us. One of our men stood looking at it a while ago and pointed out that it isn't perfectly built. Some of the stones are crooked. The lines don't all run true.

And he was right. It isn't perfect.

But for me, that wall is the church.

This month we are going to look at what it means to let yourself be built into the church of God. What does that look like? What does it feel like? Is it a bit uncomfortable? Is it a bit of a challenge?

Yes. It is.

But the worth of Christ growing inside you is so much greater than the discomfort.

## Blessed are the hungry

The first verse I want to read is Matthew 5:6.

*"Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be satisfied."*

My wife read it for us this morning in Afrikaans. One translation says *geseënd*, another says *gelukkig* — blessed, or happy. Both are true, and both are worth sitting with.

Here is the thing. If you want to be part of the church — if Jesus is building us together as one congregation — it is not always an easy process. You don't always want to do what the Lord wants done. You don't always want to be in that position.

You don't always want to be next to this living stone, or that one.

But it is God who places you.

And one of the important things — I almost want to say essential things — is that a hunger for righteousness is built up in us.

So what does a hunger for righteousness look like? What is righteousness here?

## One. What a hunger for righteousness looks like

> *It looks like someone who lets himself be led, be cared for, and be grown up — instead of fighting for his own way.*

### A see-saw is not a relationship

If my relationship with the Lord has to be a fight all the time about who comes first — the Lord or me — then I am going to spend my whole life stopping and starting. I will always feel I have to prove my point to Him, and that He has to prove His point to me.

You know how it is. In high school the boys start a lot of relationships. You know the family, you like the girl a bit, and you think that is love. But you don't really know how it works, because you have only just fallen in love. Then you start finding out who she actually is, and that is when the fighting starts.

That isn't love. That is you discovering how you handle love. How do I learn to love someone?

And when you grow up, you reach a place we call faithfulness. We call it commitment.

That is what the church is.

But when we sit in a place and we don't understand what church is, or what church is going to do with me, then it starts to feel like that early kind of relationship. I push and I push and I still don't come up.

It is a see-saw.

And heaven help the man whose partner on the other end weighs more than he does. That is not going to work, no matter how hard he tries. He will sit on the ground and stay there.

Do you know what he has to do? He has to invite someone else to climb on with him.

A relationship with the Lord is not a see-saw, where one of us has to go down for the other to come up. A relationship with the Lord is a hunger for His righteousness.

### What a baby cannot do for himself

Look at what righteousness is through a father and a mother and their children.

A child cannot look after himself. He cannot tell you what is wrong. He cries — and he is either wet, or something hurts, or he is sick. That is all he has. He is built to protest, whatever the trouble is.

In our relationship with the Lord we often end up in exactly that place. We feel like a child. We don't know what is wrong. So we need to be led.

We need a father and a mother standing over us, showing us what is actually going to work here. It is not about what I want, or what I know, or what I feel like having. I need to be led by someone who stands over me.

And as I am taught what God's will is and what is good for me, I learn — like an older child — how to care, and what to do.

There is a wisdom in parents who love each other and decide to bring a child into a home where they are safe and happy. That is a picture of the church.

But often it isn't a healthy picture, because we know we only feel the need to come to church when we are not doing well.

That is not what this is. A family is always together.

The church is not a place you go to collect something. It is a family where people carry one another.

### Don't wait until the nappies are clean

When you sit in the church and you are being taught, the Holy Spirit eventually makes you hungry. God's own desire wakes up inside you to live a different life.

That is why you sit among leaders. Not to be controlled — to be led safely into what is healthy, so that you don't spend years walking around in your pain and in old lies without ever realising what is actually going on.

Now think about a nappy being changed.

There is shame in it. Something is exposed that you would rather nobody saw. But the same hands that take the dirty nappy off are the hands that put the powder on afterwards.

That is how leaders have to be.

When somebody wants to change, and the leaders come to help, they are going to see the mess. They are going to see the shame. And they must not walk away when they see it. They must care for it.

Don't wait until the nappies are clean before you love someone.

I wondered for a long time what kind of father I would be. I really wondered about it. And then I watched my wife with our child, and it was the sweetest time of my life — watching her choose to build that little person, day after day.

These little people who are growing are precious. At some point you stop looking at the problem, and you start seeing what God is forming.

### Able is not the same as responsible

When you grow up and you carry responsibility for someone, you see things differently. You experience things differently. Yes, sometimes you are tired because you didn't sleep. And you keep going anyway.

And as you take responsibility, the Lord builds you. He develops gifts in you so that you can help people step out of what the enemy is holding them in, and help them stand with character — so that we can look each other in the face and love each other under any circumstances.

Romans 1:16 says: *"I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes."*

What is the power of God? In Greek the word is **dynamis**.

Let me show you what that means with a picture.

There is a child I know whose legs have just got long enough to reach the pedals of a car and press them. She can see over the dashboard.

So — she is able to work the car.

But should she be driving it yet? Should she be handed the responsibility for that car?

No.

Being able and being responsible are not the same thing. The power of God is not a switch you flip because you can reach it. It is God working in a life over time, and it comes with faith, and it comes with being carried while you grow.

### Nobody makes the food alone

Have you ever watched the older people when they get together to prepare food? Everyone is busy with the vegetables. You are cutting, you are peeling, you are working — and none of you is making the meal on your own.

It is not one person. It is not just mama in the kitchen while everyone else waits to eat.

That is what we are supposed to look like. People preparing together. Getting hungry together. Facing the problems together. And trusting the direction we are being moved in together.

And God grows among us.

So we don't have to be ashamed. The Lord has washed me with His blood. But I still have to live close to my brother and my sister in the Lord.

You cannot love someone in one hour on a Sunday.

I remember riding my bicycle over the koppies in Pretoria — that run of hills people call the boerewors curtain — just to go and visit someone. The whole way there I knew exactly what it was costing me, and I was willing to spend it.

That is what love does. It plants a willingness in you to get to know someone for who God knows he is — and to believe the best of him.

Even when he is not at his best.

It is not comfortable. But that is love.

## Two. Be willing to sacrifice

> *A hunger for the Lord costs you something. If it never costs you anything, it isn't hunger — it is an appetite for an experience.*

The second thing, if you want to be hungry for the Lord, is that you have to be willing to give something up.

Church is not an experience. We don't come to get a feeling.

### The sweets in my pocket

Let me give you an example, and my children are a very good example of this.

If I walk into a shop with five rand in my pocket, what am I going to choose? One sweet. One rand's worth, carefully.

But if I walk into that same shop with a hundred rand in my pocket, what happens? I walk out with eighty rand of sweets.

That is how the flesh works. The more you get, the more your thinking changes. I start thinking more of myself and less of other people.

Isn't that true?

But there is something else built in — and I want to say it is by the Holy Spirit. There is a love that is willing to sacrifice more than it receives.

Why? Because you are already full. What the Lord gives you is always more than what you asked Him for.

### My oldest brother

I am one of four brothers. And for years I was at odds with my oldest brother.

Do you know how it started? I had decided that he had to follow my lead before there could be peace. I was at war with his heart. The things he had done, the things I had seen — I read all of it as arrogance.

For years I prayed about him.

And the Lord did not begin by telling me what my brother had to do.

The Lord said to me: *Do you have a right to that?*

And then He said: *Be quiet.*

I said, how do I do that? I always answered back. I was a proper Jack Russell — somebody says something and I am already barking.

The Lord said the hardest thing He could have said. *Be quiet. I am going to teach you who I see in your brother. And if you have any appreciation for Me, you will not judge him.*

Paul writes in 2 Corinthians 12:10: *"For the sake of Christ, then, I am content with weaknesses, insults, hardships, persecutions, and calamities. For when I am weak, then I am strong."*

Do you know where the Holy Spirit is trying to get you? To the place where you give in.

Why? Because that is where peace is — peace with whatever comes down your road. You are no longer busy with what you want to get out of this. You are focused on the peace of God.

Can we do that on our own? No. I need you. You need me.

And when the Lord builds me into the church, do you know what great peace that gives me? People stop seeing my mistakes. They stop thinking of me as a mistake. They see me as the work of the Lord.

I don't know about you, but I need people to see me as the work of the Lord.

Do you know why? Because I have a lot of mistakes.

## Three. The righteousness that makes you new

> *God's righteousness does not only forgive you. It changes you — and it shows up in what you refuse to bow to, and in what you are willing to look at without turning away.*

The third thing I want to stand still on is the righteousness that changes me and makes me new. I want to give you two pictures.

### They would not bow

The first is Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.

The king said they had to bow. We usually read that as a story about courage. I think we need to open the door a bit wider than that — not just to be inspired by it, but to understand it.

Those men knew something. They knew that if they bowed even once, it would begin to silence their hunger for the righteousness of God.

They had grown up under a law where one wrong thing meant you had to go and bring a sacrifice to put it right. In the Old Testament, if you did wrong, you paid. That was not a question of *if*. It was a question of *how*.

So they knew exactly what bowing would cost them on the inside.

And they said to the king: even if our God does not rescue us, we will not bow. They were on their way to the fire — they knew their flesh was going to burn.

Then the king turned to his men and asked: didn't we throw three men in there?

Because there was a fourth one standing in the fire with them.

That is what this is about. Standing together with Jesus.

When a hunger for the Lord is cultivated in you, you don't bow. Because when you bow, your heart bows with you, and your faith bows with you.

And if you are struggling to stand — ask your brother to pray with you. Ask your brother to come and stand with you.

### The One who did not turn away

The last picture is Jesus on the cross.

We mostly picture a neat cross. The actual physical reality is something we need to look at properly.

The Word says He had no form or beauty that we should look at Him. He was so disfigured that the people watching turned their faces away and said: that was not a man.

And do you know what Jesus was saying by letting His body be ruined like that?

*Every time people have turned away from you without mercy — I saw you. I saw your pain. I am the One who did not turn away from the will of God. I let myself be exposed. I let myself be broken.*

So that whoever believes in Him — John 3:16 — will not perish.

We have been working through the subject of offence. Here is the connection. The offence you are carrying is the thing you have to bring and lay down for your brother and your sister. Your judgment of him too.

Because he is also a child of the Lord.

Galatians 6:1 says: *"Brothers, if anyone is caught in any transgression, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit of gentleness. Keep watch on yourself, lest you too be tempted."*

Notice the second half. When you step into someone else's weakness, you are not the better one. The Word tells you to watch yourself while you are standing there.

## Where it begins

The Lord wants to build us in. He wants to make us strong.

But our idea of church has to change. Our idea of what a brother and a sister are has to change. Our idea of how this is done has to change.

Because there are going to be people who are hungry. People who want to change. People who are looking for the Lord.

And if we cannot show them the love of God, they will walk straight past us.

So where does it begin?

It begins with me. I am touched. I change. I give myself. I say: Lord, change me first.
