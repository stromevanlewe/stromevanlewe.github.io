# Hunger for Righteousness

*Hendrik van der Merwe · 4 October 2026*

I want to talk this morning about being built into the wall.

There is a wall here in the church, and it goes back a while with us. One of our men stood looking at it a while ago and pointed out that it isn't perfectly built. Some of the stones are crooked. The lines don't all run true.

And he was right. It isn't perfect.

But for me, that wall is the church.

This month we are going to look at what it means to let yourself be built into the church of God. What does that look like? What does it feel like? Is it a bit uncomfortable? Is it a bit of a challenge?

Yes. It is.

But the worth of Christ growing inside you is so much greater than the discomfort.

## Blessed are the hungry

The first verse I want to read is Matthew 5:6.

*"Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness, for they shall be satisfied."*

Here is the thing. If you want to be part of the church — if Jesus is building us together as one congregation — it is not always an easy process. You don't always want to do what the Lord wants done. You don't always want to be in that position.

You don't always want to be next to this living stone, or that one.

But it is God who places you.

And when Jesus makes you part of the church, He builds you in. As you make the choices — to change, to grow — He makes you hungry for what He is doing.

And one of the important things — I almost want to say essential things — is that a hunger for righteousness is built up in us.

So what does a hunger for righteousness look like? What is righteousness here?

## One. What a hunger for righteousness looks like

> *It looks like someone who lets himself be led, be cared for, and be grown up — instead of fighting for his own way.*

### A see-saw is not a relationship

If my relationship with the Lord has to be a fight all the time about who comes first — the Lord or me — then I am going to spend my whole life stopping and starting. I will always feel I have to prove my point to Him, and that He has to prove His point to me.

You know how it is. In high school the boys start a lot of relationships. You know the family, you like the girl a bit, and you think that is love. But you don't really know how it works, because you have only just fallen in love. Then you start finding out who she actually is, and that is when the fighting starts.

That isn't love. That is you discovering how you handle love. How do I learn to love someone?

And when you grow up, you reach a place we call faithfulness. We call it commitment.

That is what the church is.

But when we sit in a place and we don't understand what church is, or what church is going to do with me, then it starts to feel like that early kind of relationship. I push and I push and I still don't come up.

It is a see-saw.

And heaven help the man whose partner on the other end weighs more than he does. That is not going to work anyway.

Then you have to invite someone else to climb on with him.

A relationship with the Lord is not a see-saw, where one of us has to go down for the other to come up. A relationship with the Lord is a hunger for His righteousness.

### What a baby cannot do for himself

Look at what righteousness is through a father and a mother and their children.

A child cannot look after himself. He cannot tell you what is wrong. He cries — and he is either wet, or something hurts, or he is sick. That is all he has. He is built to protest, whatever the trouble is.

In our relationship with the Lord we often end up in exactly that place. We feel like a child. We don't know what is wrong. So we need to be led.

We need a father and a mother standing over us, showing us what is actually going to work here. It is not about what I want, or what I know, or what I feel like having. I need to be led by someone who stands over me.

And as I am taught what God's will is and what is good for me, I learn — like an older child — how to care, and what to do.

There is a wisdom in parents who love each other — whose love has grown to the point where they want to bring one more person into that upbringing, where that child can be happy and safe.

Amen. That is the picture of being the church.

But often it isn't a healthy picture, because we know we only feel the need to come to church when we are not doing well.

Who has that way of coming to church? We know it. But that is not what it is.

A family is always together, isn't it?

### They don't worry about it — they care for it

When you sit in the church and you are being taught, the Holy Spirit makes you hungry. It is God's own desire waking up inside you to have a different life.

So now He sits you among leaders. To do what? To guide you safely — to lead you into what is healthy. Amen.

So that we don't have to go through all the pain and all these lies. Because sometimes we cannot even identify the lies yet. We don't realise it.

Then the Lord puts me in a family, among people who can watch over me, people who will look out for me, people who will sometimes say to me: hey, that nappy needs changing.

But it's good. Because they are not going to leave the powder off either. It is good. It is part of all of us.

And a baby doesn't even know about shame yet.

But then the enemy comes. And you want to change, and now the leaders want to help, and they see the nappy is off and they see the shame.

They don't worry about it. They care for it.

Engela — I didn't really know how to be a father. You must have wondered about that story. I knew I *could* be a father. What I wondered was what kind of father I could be.

But it was so beautiful to me, watching her. That nappy time was the sweetest time for me. The way she handled that little one. And later I was the same — you wait until that little bottom is clean and washed, and then you kiss it.

Do you hear what I am saying? I mustn't be crude.

But it is so precious. This little person who is growing like that is so precious. You don't look at the other stuff. You don't look at those things.

There is an embracing that the Lord puts inside you, so that when you are grown and you are standing at the point of caring for someone, you see things differently.

You experience things differently.

Yes, sometimes you get tired, because you don't sleep. And you go through it anyway.

*Blessed are those who hunger for righteousness.* Amen.

### Able is not the same as responsible

And as you take responsibility, the Lord builds you. He awakens a gift in you so that you can teach people to step out of what the enemy is holding them in, and establish them with character, through problems — so that we can look each other in the face and have love in all circumstances.

Isn't that amazing?

Romans 1:16 says: *"I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes."*

Let me read it again. *I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes.*

Now what is the power of God? And I think we can stand still here too, with: all right, what do I understand the power of God to be?

There is a child I know whose legs have just got long enough to reach the pedals in my car and press them. She has eyes to see.

So — yes, she is tall enough to start the car.

But should she drive the car? Should she be placed in responsibility for the car?

No.

Let us read it in English: *For I am not ashamed of the gospel, for it is the power of God for salvation to everyone who believes, to the Jew first and also to the Greek.*

So let us look at this power. The word here in the Greek is **dynamis** — and it is faith. It is a working of something together.

### Nobody makes the food alone

You have seen it when the older people get together — everybody is busy processing the vegetables. Have you seen that? Did you grow up with that?

It is so good. This one is busy with this, and you cut, and you work, and nobody is making the food on his own.

But that day the grandfathers and the grandmothers are together, and everybody can work with people.

When we put our hearts together to walk this road together, then our focus comes off ourselves.

The gospel comes first. The message comes first. Our encouragement of one another comes first.

And then we come into line, into focus. Because our focus is so often on ourselves. But when we turn our hearts towards the gospel, towards the message, towards faith, then the Lord puts our focus on what matters for this moment.

And it is almost like that family processing the food.

It is not just one person. It is not just mama in the kitchen the whole time while everyone else waits to eat. It is people who prepare the food together, and get hungry together, and face things together — like that father and that mother who do not worry about the mess, and that baby who is in effect saying: I am wide open, I cannot handle this situation, I cannot handle this nappy. But together we work it through, and we believe in the direction we are moving in.

And God grows among us. Amen.

So we are not ashamed of it. We don't need to be any more. The Lord came and washed me with His blood. But I have to live in nearness to my brother and my sister in the Lord — and time-wise, I cannot love someone in only one hour on a Sunday.

I don't know whether you people know love.

I used to travel over the boerewors curtain in Pretoria — that ridge of hills — to go and visit someone. Because do you know what? I was fully aware of what it was costing me, and I was willing to put the effort in.

And do you know what the Lord does? When the Lord cleans you out, He plants a willingness to love in you — a willingness to get to know someone for who God knows he is, and to believe the best of him.

Even when he is not at his best yet.

It is not comfortable. Amen.

## Two. Be willing to sacrifice

> *A hunger for the Lord costs you something. If it never costs you anything, it isn't hunger — it is an appetite for an experience.*

Another important thing, if you want to be hungry for the Lord — and this is very, very important — is to sacrifice.

It is not an experience. We don't just come to church in order to experience something. Because do you know what an experience does? It puts you in the mindset of wanting something for yourself.

### The sweets in my pocket

Let me give you an example, and my children are a very good example of this.

So if I walk to the shop with five rand, what am I going to choose? One rand's worth of sweets.

But if I walk to that shop with a hundred rand, and I don't have to buy food — only sweets — what am I going to buy? Those nice round chocolate balls.

That is how the flesh is. That is how the flesh is. Say it with me: *that is how the flesh is.*

The more you get, the more my thinking changes. Then I think more of myself and less of the other person. Isn't that true?

But there is something else built in — and I want to say it is by the Holy Spirit. There is a love that is willing to sacrifice more than it receives.

Why? Why is it so? Listen carefully.

There is an inherent gift from the Lord, that when you receive His love you give more than you receive. Why?

Listen carefully — because you are full.

### My oldest brother

For years I fought with my oldest brother. Do you know why? I thought he had to follow me before there could be peace.

I was at war with his heart. The things he had seen, or the things he had done, I read as arrogance — without ever asking: Lord, make me love him, and make me understand him.

And do you know how long that took?

The Lord did not begin with me telling him what he had to do. The Lord said to me — are you ready for this? — *Be quiet.*

I said, Lord, how do I do that? Listen carefully: I always answered back. I was a proper Jack Russell. You know back-chat. You remember back-chat?

The Lord said the hardest thing to me. *Be quiet.* I said, Lord, how do I do that?

And the Lord said: *I am going to teach you who I see your brother is. And then, if you have appreciation for Me, you are not going to judge him. You are going to keep your mouth shut.*

That is beautiful, isn't it? Amen.

So the verse I want to read there is 2 Corinthians 12:10. Remember I said: more sacrifice.

*"For the sake of Christ, then, I am content with weaknesses, insults, hardships, persecutions, and calamities. For when I am weak, then I am strong."*

For the sake — for the sake of Jesus. Amen.

When we come in here into the church and I want to worship, do you know where the Holy Spirit wants to get you? At the place where you have to surrender. Why?

Because then you will have peace with whatever comes down your road. Amen.

Because you're not going to be intent on getting what you want. You're going to be focused on the peace of God.

Can we do that on our own? No. I need you. You need me.

So we have to sacrifice. We have to know that as I grow in the Lord, and I grow in the church, and the Lord builds me into the wall — do you know what great peace you have? Nobody will see my mistakes, and nobody will think of me as a mistake. They will see me as the work of the Lord.

I don't know about you, but I want people to see me as the work of the Lord.

Do you know why? Because I have a lot of mistakes. A lot of mistakes.

## Three. The righteousness that makes you new

> *God's righteousness does not only forgive you. It changes you — and it shows up in what you refuse to bow to, and in what you are willing to look at without turning away.*

The third thing about having a hunger, and what is important about having a hunger — I want to mention two examples. Because this righteousness, the Lord who changes me and the Lord who makes me new: we sometimes hold it off. Two beautiful examples that build our faith.

### They would not bow

The first is Shadrach, Meshach and Abednego.

The king said they had to bow. We usually read that as a story about courage. I think we need to open the door a bit wider than that — not just to be inspired by it, but to understand it.

Those men knew something. They knew that if they bowed even once, it would begin to silence their hunger for the righteousness of God.

They knew — the Jews knew — they had a great many things to do, they had the law to keep. And if they did one wrong thing, they knew they had to go and bring a sacrifice to put it right.

So what did they learn? Do the right thing.

That is the one thing about the Old Testament: if you lived wrongly, you were going to pay. That was not a question of *when*. It was a question of *how*.

So they knew what bowing in front of that image would cost them when it came back on them.

And there was something else they knew. If I do not bow — why would my God not stand by me? Why would my God not stand by me?

And do you know what Jesus decided?

They said to the king: even if our God does not save us. Because you know, you are sending us off there to the braai fires. Our flesh is going to burn.

And what did Nebuchadnezzar see in that fire? He turned to his companions — do you know that word? It is a lovely word, isn't it? — and said: didn't we send three men in there?

There is a son of a king standing in that fire.

That is what this is about. Standing together with Jesus.

When a hunger for the Lord is cultivated in you, you don't bow. Because when you bow, your heart bows with you, and your faith bows with you.

And if you are struggling to stand — ask your brother to pray with you. Ask your brother to come and stand with you.

### The One who did not turn away

The last picture is Jesus on the cross.

We mostly picture a neat cross. The actual physical reality is something we need to look at properly.

The Word says He had no form or beauty that we should look at Him. He was so disfigured that the people watching turned their faces away and said: that was not a man.

And do you know what Jesus was saying by letting His body be ruined like that?

*Every time people have turned away from you without mercy — I saw you. I saw your pain. I am the One who did not turn away from the will of God. I let myself be exposed. I let myself be broken.*

So that whoever believes in Him — John 3:16 — will not perish.

We have been working through the subject of offence. Did you know this about the sacrifice? When you feel offended, you have to bring it and lay it down in front of your brother and your sister — and in front of his judgement of it. Because he is also a child of the Lord. Trust that he might give you something new that turns your mind.

Do you hear what I am saying?

Galatians 6:1 says: *"Brothers, if anyone is caught in any transgression, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit of gentleness. Keep watch on yourself, lest you too be tempted."*

Do you know what? And I think many times we don't realise this. When you step into someone else's weakness, then you are not the good one — because the Word says: watch yourself too.

## Where it begins

That means the Lord wants to build us in. He wants to make us strong.

But our idea of church has to change. Our idea of what a brother and a sister are has to change. Our idea of how this is done has to change.

Because do you know what? There are going to be people who are hungry, people who want to change, people who are looking for the Lord — and if we cannot show them, they are going to walk past us.

Where does it begin?

I become hungry. I change. I give myself. I say: Lord, I change myself first.

## Let us close our eyes

I want to ask you something this morning.

If you are saying this morning: Lord, change my thoughts and my heart about what church is, about what love is — change me about what the family of the church is.

If you are saying this morning: Lord, I need a hunger and a thirst for the Lord's righteousness. So often I don't even sacrifice. I think it is only about me.

If Jesus died for the church, and out of that believers stand up to follow Jesus and to carry their cross — and if for you this has been about your own need, then I want to ask you.

If you want to say: Lord, I really want to know what hunger and thirst for righteousness is. I really want my life built up in the family of the Lord. I really want to know God's presence as a person. I really want to overcome myself, the pain, the past.

If you say: Lord, You lead the way — even if you have already given your heart to the Lord, that is not the point this morning.

If you want to say: Lord, I want to hunger and thirst for Your righteousness — just put your hand up.

And before I pray. You know how it is when you feel the Lord speaking to you. Not the microphone, not the speaker. When the Lord speaks to you, you feel a burning, or you feel your heartbeat moving faster.

That is the Lord's voice.

If you feel that this morning, just put your hand up in the air.

I am putting my hand up too.

If you say: Lord, I don't want to live without You.
