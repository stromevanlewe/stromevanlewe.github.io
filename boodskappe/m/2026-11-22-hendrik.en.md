# Stick to the Plan

*Hendrik van der Merwe · 22 November 2026*

If I can put a theme to the Word this morning, it is this: **stick to the plan.**

The sisters who read their testimonies this morning show it to us beautifully. One says it straight out: *I struggle.* And yet you hear that voice of faith in her. She is not the finished product, but she is staying right here. Another tells of a vision God has planted in her heart for the young people. And another tells of a shower — I will come back to that.

Stick to the plan.

## He was led into the wilderness

> *"Jesus left the Jordan River full of the Holy Spirit. He was led by the Spirit into the wilderness, where the devil tempted Him for forty days. He ate nothing the whole time and was very hungry."*
> Luke 4:1-2

Look carefully at what it says. He left the *water.* He comes out of the Jordan full of the Spirit, and the first place the Spirit takes Him is the dry country.

Israel also came out of a house — Egypt. And that house taught the nation to look at other gods, to give their hearts to the things around them. In Egypt there were many gods.

Look at any of the old empires, and look honestly at our own time: you can serve whatever god you want. You can serve a god you can make an image of, that you can desire, that you can chase.

Some men make money into a bit of a god. *If I just have that much, I will have arrived.* Others make a person into an idol. *If I can just get a wife like that, if I can just get a job like that, then I will start living.*

But when we look at Jesus, He is the example. **Jesus is our standard.** It is no longer me, and what I can do, and what I desire, and my solutions. The Word says He is the way, the truth and the life.

And when the Lord fills us with His love, there is already a confidence that I will overcome my sin. As we change, Jesus comes into us. The Word becomes alive, and the Holy Spirit leads us to places where we could not overcome before.

But why?

Why would the Lord take His people through a wilderness to reach a promised land? We do not understand this concept. In our own flesh we don't understand why we go through tough times.

## God goes out ahead

Let's look at Psalm 68.

A while ago I said it feels to me as though our congregation is standing in these few verses right now. There are many challenges. Where do we go from here? But it is not only for us. People come with a need, and there is something God wants to lead them into that will give them victory.

> *"O God, when You went out before Your people, when You marched through the wilderness, the earth shook; the heavens also dropped rain at the presence of God; Sinai itself was moved at the presence of God, the God of Israel."*
> Psalm 68:7-8

Listen carefully. Who is it that marches through?

With Jesus, the Holy Spirit led *Him* into the wilderness. Here it says: **God Himself went out ahead of His people, into the wilderness.**

That tells us something important happens in a wilderness. In pressure. In questions I have no answer for.

And it tells us God meets us *inside* the trouble. It is not God looking from a distance with binoculars, testing whether a swear word might slip out. It's not that. It is something deeper.

### The earth shook

As people we are very fixed on the creation. We are fixed on the things we know, the things we trust, out of our past, out of what we were taught, out of our own wisdom.

And the Lord has to get us away from those elements. He has to get us away from what our heart is set on, from where our trust lies, from what our vision is.

Why? Because without knowing it, we are tied down. We follow things because we have our own motives.

For much of what happens in our lives you already have answers. You do things because you are really **your own saviour.**

Someone gets hurt, and then he goes and learns taekwondo, because then he can defend himself. Someone thinks: if I dress like this, people will accept me. Not everyone is like that, but there is an unhealthy balance in us.

And then it says here: the earth shook.

Let me tell you — when Jesus comes into you, when His love comes into you, the first thing that happens is that you **shake.** Your certainty about what you knew, about how you thought it had to be, changes.

But it is also a responsibility. Jesus' love is a responsibility. That is why it matters that we listen to one another's testimonies. We want to ask: are you still standing in your faith? Do you see the things happening that the Lord promised? And if you do not see them happening, then you ask: Lord, why not? What is wrong?

You see, **in the wilderness the Lord comes nearer to you than you thought He could be.**

Many times we want to invite the Lord into our hearts because we realise we need to be saved, but then we keep a little lifeboat back for ourselves — our own way of doing things. We are saving up for our own honour. We are saving up to have the glory.

And we have to realise one thing. We have a fight against an enemy who will not let us go if the Lord does not reign over us.

We're sitting with an enemy that will not let go if God does not reign over us.

That means that in my surrender, in my faith, in my obedience, I must do more than only hear what I am supposed to do. Because God moves out ahead of me, and because He gives me an example in the church, He shows me through love: the person who says *I struggle* is allowed to walk forward.

That must be the standard. **Not a standard of doing things right, but a standard of seeking Jesus in spite of the problems.**

I am not going to stop. And now I am learning: it's a daily process. It's not being somewhere, it's being with Him.

And when He is with you, you discover He is the answer to everything.

How many times, before we ask for help, do you already have the answer in your head? We are reactive towards our problems. I want to be a problem solver. I know what has to happen, Lord. I have been thinking about this dream of mine for years.

And then the Lord comes and says: no, we are not going to do it that way. Walk behind Me.

And how does a person feel then?

## The heavens dropped rain

The earth shook, and the heavens dropped rain.

The water that was taken away from you — the Lord will provide for that.

What food did the Lord drop in the wilderness? Manna. To this day I wonder what that manna looked like.

But the manna is no longer there. What is there now?

Jesus says to His people: **I am the bread of life. Whoever comes to Me will never hunger, and whoever believes in Me will never thirst.** *(John 6:35)*

That Jordan River was a place of provision. But God is not only a place of provision. **He creates the provision. He is the source of it.** He is greater than provision, greater than our dreams.

And what is He waiting for? He is waiting to see how your heart behaves towards Him. In love?

Because if the Lord does not stir up love in you to follow Him, you will meet resistance in your own thinking.

> *"You, O God, sent a plentiful rain, whereby You confirmed Your inheritance when it was weary."*
> Psalm 68:9

There is a very great key lying here.

If you follow the Lord and it goes hard and you do not have all the answers — there is a God of an inheritance. There is a God who has a plan for your life.

And do you know what? You have to share this plan with Jesus. Jesus must be the guide and the direction of the plan. **He must be this life, not me.**

When I come to faith and I follow Jesus, the devil is not such a big problem to me. If I believe Jesus and I love Him, then the greater problem lies with *me* when I stop following, *me* when I stop being obedient — rather than with the devil who might keep me from what the Lord wants to give me.

Why? Because He says you are in the palm of His hand, and no one will take you out of it. But there is a relationship, and there is an acquittal, and there is a new way of seeing. When I am in relationship with the Lord, I see life differently.

### The taxi story

At one stage I was very fond of the men who drive taxis. *Very* fond of them.

No, really.

My wife talked to me about this for a long time. At one point I even started stopping at the filling stations. Then I would say to the driver: "Do you know how irresponsible you are? Do you have a licence? I want to see it."

I felt so strongly about those people that I was ready to stand in for them like a Peter — because their music is so loud they cannot think!

Is that God's will? No.

When you satisfy God, He will make it rain.

So what do we do?

> *"Imitate me, as I imitate Christ."*
> 1 Corinthians 11:1

And as He makes it rain — before anything physical happens to you — you will feel God's love in your heart. You will feel direction. You will understand how you need to change.

The Holy Spirit will show you. One of our sisters told this morning how she stood at a blocked shower head and the Lord spoke to her. Who was speaking to her there? Was it people?

No. She's seeking for the love of God, and God will use a shower. He will use a wall. He will use anything, because He will lead your heart to the inheritance He has for you.

And in the meantime you will overcome your sins — the things that keep you away from the Lord.

## There they settled

> *"Your congregation dwelt in it; You, O God, provided from Your goodness for the poor."*
> Psalm 68:10

Notice where that happens. We are talking about the wilderness.

The Afrikaans puts it beautifully: *there Your people settled.* They anchored themselves there.

You know, when you begin walking a road with the Lord — and this is true of all of us — we are renewed. The Lord's fatherhood and His renewing in our hearts change us daily. We mature in how we think.

And then our end point is no longer my desires and what I want to do and what I want to achieve. Here on the road, here in the wilderness, here in His presence where I know He can make the desert rain, I come and say:

**Lord, all along I was actually looking for You. And where You are, that is where I want to be.**

### The prison

My example of this is the prison.

I have been ministering there for years. Before that I knew nothing about a prison.

A friend phoned me and asked whether I would go to the prison with him. His brother was there and he did not know what to say. I just had to be there for moral support.

And believe me, guys, I know it for myself. When I walked through that gate, God said to me: I want to use you here.

Friends, I was so excited. I cannot even remember what happened with that man and his brother. I was so filled.

Do you know why? Because I love God.

I did not think about the prison. It only hit me a week later: I have never been in a prison, what do I say to these people? What am I going to do? Lord, did You hear right? So I argued with Him a little.

But that was 2015 — it is eleven years now. And from the first day, however burdened and low I walk in there, when I walk out I have a song in my heart. Then I know God was involved and present, because I listened to His voice.

There is dynamics to listening to God that will change your being, to trust in who He is and not in what the desert is.

If you obey Him.

We want to bring one another to that place where we become obedient and do what the Lord says. And if we do not know God, then we ask: how do we get this love into us? How do we cultivate a way of living in which this love of the Lord becomes more and more involved in my life?

Because I live with this knowledge: **the more of His love is poured out in me, the more I will change.**

That is the important thing. Not how lost I am. Not how heavy the circumstance looks.

When God climbs into your wilderness, everything shakes. When God climbs into your wilderness, your eyes also begin to see with love the possibility of what God can do.

Let me ask a question. Is He a God who does things to make us believe, or does He make us believe in what He is going to do?

**He makes us believe in what He is going to do.** And that means we will follow God all the way.

## After you have suffered a while

> *"In His undeserved goodness God called you to share in His eternal glory in Christ. After you have suffered a little while, He Himself will restore you. He will make you steadfast, strong and firmly established."*
> 1 Peter 5:10

*In His undeserved goodness.* **We deserve nothing, guys.** We deserve nothing.

*God called you* — He calls you to have a share in Christ, to go through your hard times and make Christ the life, to put your faith on Him.

*After you have suffered a little while.*

Are you encouraged now?

I have a New Zealand friend who uses that expression: you get the man who breaks up the party. Everyone is enjoying themselves, and then he arrives. He's a buzzkill. He's killing the buzz. The mood is broken.

But let me tell you: when God fills you with faith, it is as though your ears are stopped to the people who are negative. Have you experienced that? It is as though the people who do not want to encourage you to follow God simply do not get through any more, because you are experiencing God in such a way — and my words do not come close to it — that you *know* He will come through for you. No matter what.

We have to be built up in this. Sometimes we have to wrestle with each other. Iron sharpens iron. But what comes out of it, says this verse, is courage and faith and strength and steadfastness. I am set on what God gives me.

And that is why it matters that the congregation remembers what God said to each other. When the Lord brings it to pass, the congregation must be able to testify: what was in this person was placed there by the Lord.

You look at someone you have seen in this town for years, someone you always thought of in a certain way — well, it is Boshof after all — and now the Lord is doing things with her. Wow. I want to be part of that.

Listen carefully: **you are not going to follow me because I am such a good Christian. You are going to follow me because God is alive in my life.** Because He keeps His word.

## That grey car

I want to tell you a testimony.

That grey car standing outside with a few bumps and bruises — I did not have a car. And hear me carefully, I am not talking about prosperity that God planned for me.

Someone phones me from Beaufort West, someone I have known for years, and he asks me questions. Just general questions. But this is not a man who asks questions — this is a farmer who says very little on a telephone. You know the generation of farmers: they want to know what that implement costs, and then it is "okay thanks, who is the contact person, I will see you tomorrow."

But I am having this long conversation with this farmer who is asking me questions. And he is steering the conversation slowly towards a point. Then he asks: "And how is your car?"

And I say: "No, but I don't have a car, oom."

And in that moment — hear me — in that moment God was my witness through the Holy Spirit. The Lord says to me: **this man is going to give you a car.**

My word.

I was so excited. I told my wife and the friends we were visiting that morning. Every Saturday morning we used to eat pancakes with them.

These people look at me. "Okay. When you came to visit us you must have cut something off that you had somewhere other than Boshof. But okay."

There was no conversation. Everyone just looked back at me.

But God had filled me through His Spirit. I knew.

A week later we are sitting with the same people. And this is how God works — because He wants to make people aware of His works. He wants people to know that He has the power over creation to make the earth shake so that His will comes true.

**But He is looking for believers. He is looking for people who can love Him in the wilderness.** In hard times.

That man phones me. He says: "I bought you a ticket. Next week you travel from Kimberley to Beaufort West and you come and fetch your car."

When I told those people *that*, they thought now he really has lost it.

I sat down and talked with that man. He says he prayed one morning and the Lord told him to do it. He had inherited a Mercedes from his father. A grand car. And he wrestled with the Lord: "Lord, I cannot give this Mercedes away — I waited so long for this Mercedes, something from my father."

If you know Beaufort West: he lives not far from the Checkers centre in the main street. His son had started farming and he had retired, so he walks to Checkers.

And he tells me: just before he walked into Checkers, he said, "Okay Lord, I have struggled enough. I will give that car if You want me to."

He goes in, does his shopping, pays like a good man, and walks out. On the way back a woman pulls in next to him with a car. She had put a cardboard sign in the window. Nobody knew that car was for sale. She pulls up onto the pavement.

In that moment — before she could even put it on Facebook — this man walks past.

And as he walks past, he says: *"That is the car."*

That is what our God does.

He just doesn't benefit somebody for something. When He works, people know it is Him.

People know it is Him.

And you may be struggling, because you do not want to give something up. **But He cannot move if you do not give it up.**

But you know what? His life is better than ours.

## Immeasurably more

> *"Now to Him who is able, by the power at work within us, to do immeasurably more than all we ask or think — to Him be the glory in the church and in Christ Jesus, throughout all generations."*
> Ephesians 3:20-21

Notice where verse 21 places the glory: **in the church.**

God works this thing through His church. And a church is a person who joins himself to the Lord's body in order to see God's promises come true. To bring the poor and the blind and the broken into the family of the Lord, so that God can shake their lives — their wildernesses — and bring the rain of renewal, so that people can see what God does.

That is my prayer for the new year: that we will be so close that we know what the will of God is, in spite of the sacrifices.

Because who does not make sacrifices? This whole life is a sacrifice. You start in the morning when you do not want to get up, but you know you have to work, because you have to provide for the children.

God is the only One with a reward like the reward He has for us.

## Prayer

Lord, thank You so much for Your love.

Lord, I know Boshof is a place that can often feel like a wilderness. Very often.

But I know You are in the renewing. Not only being changed — this is not a make-up session. It's a renewing session.

People feel like they are dragged through tough times. But our testimony is that God stands up for us. He stands up inside us. He stands up inside our problems, and He shows Himself to us and gives us the privilege of testifying about Him.

What a privilege.

Lord, many people want to be in Boshof in order to get *away.* For many it is only a halfway station.

But Lord, I pray that You will make Boshof a place to live for Your church. A place for people to stand up and talk and sing and shout about God and about His changes.

Everyone who trusts the Lord can do this.

Lord, we bless ourselves in Jesus' Name. Amen.
